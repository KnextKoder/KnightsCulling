# KNIGHT'S CULLING

The Kingdom is overrun, the King is desperate, and you... are on a strict budget.
Armed with the legendary "Dragon Blood" blade, you are the realm's last hope. But ancient power isn't free. Every swing of your relic costs cold, hard cash.
Combat with a Cost: Every strike drains your purse.
Resource Management: Scavenge for coins to keep the fight alive.
Extinction Event: Clear the floors, slay the dinos, and don't go broke trying.
Can you save the Kingdom without filing for bankruptcy?
